Cut a café stop or an auto-pause out of a ride

The recording kept running while you stood still. Cut that stretch out, or the dead start and end of an activity.

The problem

You pulled over at a café or a viewpoint and the tracking carried on. A watch keeps recording a hike while you sit. A bike computer logs the walk to the restaurant as riding at a very slow speed, with no cadence and no power. That stretch drags down your average speed, inflates the elapsed time and clutters the map.

The steps

  1. Open the file from Files, AirDrop or the share sheet.
  2. Choose Remove Section.
  3. Drag the handles on the chart to choose the section to remove. You can adjust the selection at any time.
  4. Check the numbers under After applying in the preview.
  5. Tap Apply.
  6. Save or share the file.

Everything inside the section goes, hidden data included. FitFitr will not cut out the whole file: it asks you to narrow the range.

The result

The stretch is gone from the file. Distance, time and averages are recalculated for the laps and the whole session.

Share it

Save it to Files as a new file or replace the original, send it through the share sheet, or upload it to Strava. If the activity is already on Strava, Strava rejects the new file as a duplicate: delete the original there first, then upload.
