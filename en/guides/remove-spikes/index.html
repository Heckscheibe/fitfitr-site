Remove heart-rate or power spikes from a .fit file

A strap, a cadence sensor or a power meter reported something that cannot be true. Find those readings on the chart, then remove them or replace them.

The problem

A heart-rate strap with a low battery reports 230 bpm for a few seconds. A cadence magnet slips. A power meter shows 2000 watts for six seconds. One bad reading raises your maximum and drags your average, on every lap and for the whole session.

The steps

  1. Open the file. Import it from Files, through AirDrop, or from the share sheet of the app it is in.
  2. Choose Remove Spikes.
  3. Choose the measurement you are editing: heart rate, power, speed or cadence.
  4. Set the threshold, or tap the chart to place it directly. The preview below follows as you go.
  5. Decide what happens to the spikes: remove them, or replace them with a value you choose.
  6. Tap Apply. You see live what changes. Undo takes it back, and the file changes only when you save.

Choose the measurement first. Each one has its own threshold.

The result

The spikes are gone. Averages and maximums change with your edit, on every lap and for the whole session. Everything else in the file is carried through unchanged.

Share it

Save it to Files as a new file or replace the original, send it through the share sheet, or upload it to Strava. If the activity is already on Strava, Strava rejects the new file as a duplicate: delete the original there first, then upload.
